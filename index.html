About tabeebway.com

tabeebway.com is a medical tourism facilitation platform helping families access multidisciplinary cancer care in India — including advanced diagnostics, specialist second opinions, and coordinated international patient support. Many families choose India for oncology treatment because of access to experienced specialists and structured care pathways.

We guide you through every step — from sharing reports and arranging a free second opinion to travel coordination and follow-up after you return home. Hospital selection is based on your clinical needs and the care plan recommended after specialist review.
