Overview

Understanding Head and Neck Cancer

What is head and neck cancer?

Head and Neck Cancer requires timely specialist evaluation. Indian tertiary centres offer diagnostics, tumour boards and protocol-based therapy for international patients.

Causes & risk factors

Risk factors vary by cancer type and may include age, genetics, infections, lifestyle and environmental exposures. Your oncologist will interpret your personal risk context.

Why early diagnosis matters

Earlier detection often enables more options, shorter treatment and better recovery planning for travel home.

Symptoms

Common Warning Signs

These symptoms do not always mean cancer — but they deserve specialist review.

  • Unexplained weight loss or fatigue
  • Persistent pain or swelling
  • Bleeding or unusual discharge
  • Lumps or lymph node swelling
  • Breathing, swallowing or neurological changes (site-specific)
  • Symptoms that do not improve

Diagnosis

How Head and Neck Cancer Is Diagnosed

  • Specialist consultation
  • Blood tests and tumour markers when relevant
  • Imaging (CT / MRI / PET as indicated)
  • Biopsy / histopathology
  • Molecular tests when useful
  • Staging work-up
