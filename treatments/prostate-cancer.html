Overview

Understanding Prostate Cancer

What is prostate cancer?

Prostate Cancer requires timely specialist evaluation. Indian tertiary centres offer diagnostics, tumour boards and protocol-based therapy for international patients.

Causes & risk factors

Risk factors vary by cancer type and may include age, genetics, infections, lifestyle and environmental exposures.

Why early diagnosis matters

Earlier detection often enables more options, shorter treatment and better recovery planning for travel home.

Symptoms

Common Warning Signs

  • Unexplained weight loss or fatigue
  • Persistent pain or swelling
  • Bleeding or unusual discharge
  • Lumps or lymph node swelling
  • Site-specific symptoms that do not improve
  • Night sweats or fever (selected cancers)

Diagnosis

How Prostate Cancer Is Diagnosed

  • Specialist consultation
  • Blood tests and tumour markers when relevant
  • Imaging (CT / MRI / PET as indicated)
  • Biopsy / histopathology
  • Molecular tests when useful
  • Staging work-up
